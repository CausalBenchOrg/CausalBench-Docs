# Licensing, service terms, and community rules

Different rules apply to the CausalBench software, the hosted service, and each contributed artifact. One does not replace the others.

!!! important
    This page summarizes the project-provided terms in this documentation and was last reviewed on September 21, 2026. Terms presented during account registration or publication may be more current and govern use of the hosted service. Contact `support@causalbench.org` when rights or retention requirements are material to your project.

## CausalBench software

The CausalBench source code is distributed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/CausalBenchOrg/CausalBench/blob/main/LICENSE). That license governs use, modification, and distribution of the software itself. It does not automatically grant rights to datasets, model weights, papers, or other third-party content available through the registry.

## Hosted service

The project-provided service policy requires users to:

- comply with applicable national and international laws;
- use the hosted CausalBench service for open-source research; and
- avoid illegal, unethical, abusive, or disruptive activity that harms the service, its users, or its collaborative environment.

These service conditions are separate from the Apache license on the software. If you need to use the hosted registry outside this scope, obtain clarification from the service operator first.

## Licenses on components and data

Every dataset, model, metric, and other contributed artifact can have its own source license or usage restrictions. Before downloading, executing, adapting, or redistributing one:

1. review its source, URL, license, and citation metadata;
2. confirm that your intended use is permitted; and
3. preserve required attribution and notices.

Being publicly downloadable from CausalBench is not, by itself, evidence that an artifact is public domain or covered by the CausalBench software license.

## Uploading content

By submitting content, you affirm that you have the licenses, permissions, and rights required to share it. You remain responsible for the content and any harm it may cause.

Before uploading:

- remove credentials, private keys, access tokens, and identifying local paths;
- do not include personal, confidential, restricted, or export-controlled data without the necessary authority and safeguards;
- provide accurate source, license, and citation metadata; and
- check transitive files, generated outputs, and model artifacts—not only `config.yaml`.

The project-provided policy states that public content can be made available for public, non-commercial research use and recorded permanently. You can grant only rights that you actually hold; an upstream license or data-use agreement continues to apply. Review [reproducibility and publication](reproducibility.md#local-private-and-public-are-different-states) before making a record public.

## Community expectations

- **Give credit.** Do not take data, code, or ideas without the appropriate permission, license, and citation.
- **Do no harm.** Do not harm the service, other users, research participants, or affected communities.
- **Collaborate constructively.** Describe limitations and provenance so others can evaluate and reproduce the work.
- **Take responsibility.** Review everything you publish; public records can be permanent.

Failure to comply can result in action up to and including account suspension.
